How to play

Hoppers spawn and hop on their own — and they never stop. They hop in the direction they're facing and can't steer, so they'll cheerfully hop off a cliff into the fire. Your job is to build them a safe path in real time.

  • Pick a tool from the tray (or press 1–4).
  • Left-click the board to place it; right-click to undo the last one.
  • Plank = a bridge. Barrier = bounces hoppers back the other way. Spring = launches them up and forward in a big arc (aim it at a floating portal). Balloon = the next hopper to touch it floats up, drifting on the wind, then it pops.
  • Each portal is worth points by how hard it is to reach. Hit the target score to win — so the easy portal alone often isn't enough; combine tools and aim some hoppers at the high-value portal.
  • Hoppers pass through each other, hop off cliffs if unguided, and later levels spawn them from both sides. Space pauses, R restarts.

A web remake of Hopper (Gargle Games, 2013). Physics by planck.js.
