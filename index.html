How to play

The hopper hops on its own — up and to the right, again and again. It can't change direction, so it needs a path. Left-click in the pit to drop a plank as a stepping stone (right-click or Undo to remove the last one). When your bridge looks right, press Go. Reach the spinning portal to win; touch a spike or fall in the pit and it's back to the drawing board.

A web remake of Hopper (Gargle Games, 2013). Physics by planck.js.
